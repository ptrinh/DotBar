# DotBar Privacy Policy

DotBar does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data.

- All configuration lives locally in `~/Library/Application Support/DotBar/` (or the app's sandbox container for the App Store version).
- Scripts you configure run on your Mac. Any network requests they make (for example fetching a price from an API) are made by your scripts, to the endpoints you choose. DotBar itself makes no network requests.
- No analytics, no crash reporting, no accounts.
